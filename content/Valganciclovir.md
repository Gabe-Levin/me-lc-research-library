# Valganciclovir

Oral prodrug of ganciclovir. More potent vs [[CMV]] and [[HHV-6]] than [[Valacyclovir]], but more adverse effects (myelosuppression, GI). Used when CMV or HHV-6 dominate the viral picture.

## Studies

- **Montoya 2013 RCT** (n=30 ME/CFS with high [[EBV]] + [[HHV-6]] IgG): 6 months valganciclovir → significant improvement in cognition + mental fatigue
- Earlier Montoya pilot (n=12): 75% had "near-resolution" of viral CNS dysfunction symptoms
