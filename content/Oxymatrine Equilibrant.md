# Oxymatrine / Equilibrant

Quinolizidine alkaloid from *Sophora flavescens* root. Antiviral, anti-inflammatory, apoptosis-promoting, immunomodulatory. Equilibrant is [[Dr Chia]]'s proprietary formula.

## Why used

First-line for chronic [[Enteroviruses]] in [[ME-CFS]] (per [[Dr Chia]]). Demonstrated CVB3 protection in animal models.

## Dosing per Chia

- **Start ⅛ tablet**, ramp very slowly to 2–3 tablets BID over weeks
- Patients with severe pain → don't start (too immune-stimulating); just fatigue OK
- Side effects = increase in any pre-existing symptom (body aches, headaches, abdominal pain, nausea, diarrhea, fatigue) — shifting immune response = another battle

## Response rates (per Chia)

- Oxymatrine ~52%; Equilibrant ~56%
- 88% of patients switching oxymatrine → Equilibrant had continued improvement
- 2021 update: ~30–50% response, ~1800–2000 patients treated

## Don't use if

Autoimmune disease (immune-stimulating).

## Rifampin augmentation

**2011 Chia abstract** — 7-day rifampin 300mg BID added to oxymatrine/equilibrant: 67% develop flu-like reaction. **70% of original responders** improved further. 64% of responders able to discontinue herbs within weeks/months. Mechanism likely involves cytokine shift (↑ TNF-α + IL-1α/β mRNA during combo).

## Relapse pattern

- Men: often done after 3–6 months
- Women: typically relapse within weeks of stopping; need indefinite low-dose
