# Neuroinflammation

Microglial / astrocyte activation in the brain. Implicated as a final common pathway for fatigue, brain fog, cognitive dysfunction in [[ME-CFS]] and [[Long COVID]].

## Evidence

- **Nakatomi 2014, J Nucl Med** — PET evidence of widespread neuroinflammation in ME/CFS (first TSPO-PET study)
- Microglial activation in ME/CFS and LC
- Brain MRS: elevated cerebral lactate (Shungu 2012; Lyall 2025 Mol Psych — 7T study showing elevated lactate in pgACC + dACC)
- Persistent immune activation in LC post-acute

## Treatments

- [[Low Dose Naltrexone]] — TLR4 antagonism, microglia-targeted; Younger's preferred. **2nd-highest-rated treatment** in Eastep et al. 2025 PNAS (n=3,925 patient survey).
- [[Low Dose Rapamycin]] — direct microglial mTOR target
- [[Low Dose Abilify]] — dopamine D2 modulation; may mediate neuroinflammation
- [[Red Light Therapy]] / transcranial PBM — neuroprotective, reduces NF-κB
- [[Methylene Blue]] — crosses BBB, accumulates in neuronal mitochondria
- [[HBOT]] — anti-inflammatory; reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines, polarizes macrophages toward M2
- [[Stellate Ganglion Block]] — modulates ANS → modulates neuroimmune feedback loops
- Anti-inflammatory supplements: curcumin, resveratrol, quercetin, omega-3, melatonin

## Researchers central to this mechanism

[[Dr Younger]] — multiple papers; central researcher of neuroinflammation in ME/CFS. "Brain on Fire" 2018; "Good Day Bad Day" study; TSPO-PET work.
