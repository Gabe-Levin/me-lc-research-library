# Mitochondrial Dysfunction

Leading hypothesis for the energy impairment in [[ME-CFS]] and [[Long COVID]]. Mitochondria produce ATP via Krebs cycle + electron transport chain; disruption explains exertion-triggered crashes ([[Post-Exertional Malaise]]).

## Root cause vs collateral damage — open debate

- **ME Association 2019 review**: argues mitochondrial dysfunction is collateral damage from hypoxia, [[Oxidative Stress]], immune abnormalities, inflammation, or muscle degeneration.
- **Wirth & Scheibenbogen** (recent): propose ME is acquired mitochondrial myopathy of skeletal muscle.
- **2025 study**: dysfunction of both mitochondria *and* endoplasmic reticulum.
- Documented in [[Long COVID]] as well.

## Documented findings

- Reduced ATP production (immune cells in low-energy state — Missailidis 2020, JCI Insight)
- Mitochondrial fragmentation in endothelial cells, impaired O2 delivery (Kloc 2022, Cells)
- Shift to anaerobic glycolysis = [[Warburg Effect]]
- Metabolomic profile resembling hibernating animals (Naviaux 2016, PNAS)
- Reduced VO2 max + 2-day CPET drop (Keller 2014)
- Elevated reactive oxygen species (Paul 2021, Redox Biology)
- LC viral persistence triggers chronic oxidative stress (Neubrand 2023, Front Immunol)
- Impaired mitochondrial biogenesis: low PGC-1α (Tomas 2017)
- **2024 muscle biopsy LC study**: reduced SDH post-PEM; reduced oxidative phosphorylation capacity; TCA cycle metabolite depletion; CD68+/CD3+ infiltration

## Treatments (support framework)

### Antioxidants (reduce mitochondrial oxidative stress)
[[CoQ10]] · MitoQ · PQQ · NAC · resveratrol · ALA · liposomal glutathione

### Membrane repair
[[Phosphatidylcholine]] (oral or IV) — ATP360, NT Factor lipids, BioPC Pro

### Substrates / cofactors
L-carnitine (acetyl-L-carnitine) · [[NAD Plus]] precursors (NR, NMN) · oral NADH · D-ribose

### Krebs cycle modulators
[[Oxaloacetate]] — inhibits SDH (Krebs running in reverse during PEM) · Chrysin (also SDH inhibitor)

### "Patch" the electron transport chain
[[Methylene Blue]] — donates electrons directly, bypasses leakage

### Other
[[Red Light Therapy]] / [[Photobiomodulation]] — enhances cytochrome c oxidase
[[HBOT]] — improves mitochondrial function
[[Low Dose Rapamycin]] — increases ATP, induces mitophagy

### Clinical trials in progress (LC)
CoQ10 · MitoQ (NCT05373043) · ALA · NR (NCT05703074) · resveratrol (NCT05601180)
