# Warburg Effect

Metabolic shift from mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation → cytoplasmic glycolysis (fermentation), producing lactate. Originally described in cancer cells; documented as a feature of [[ME-CFS]] and [[Long COVID]] energy metabolism.

## In IACCs

- Naviaux 2016 metabolomics — "hibernating animal" / cell-danger response state
- Inefficient ATP production → lactate accumulation → muscle fatigue
- Exertion: tissues already in ischemic / Warburg state; Krebs cycle starts running in reverse → mitochondrial [[Oxidative Stress]] + lactic acid → delayed fatigue = [[Post-Exertional Malaise]]

## Therapeutic angle

- [[Oxaloacetate]] discovered to act as a *signaling molecule* controlling the Warburg shift (in addition to its Krebs intermediate role)
- [[Methylene Blue]] reroutes electrons in the ETC, may bypass the inefficiency
