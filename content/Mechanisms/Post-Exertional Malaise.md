# Post-Exertional Malaise (PEM)

The hallmark symptom of [[ME-CFS]] and a key feature of [[Long COVID]]. Worsening of symptoms after physical, cognitive, or emotional exertion. Symptoms typically peak 12–48h after the trigger and last days to weeks. Can be induced by sensory overload (light, sound).

## Proposed mechanisms (multi-axis)

### 1. Energy metabolism / [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]]
- Reduced ATP production
- [[Oxidative Stress]]
- Impaired mitochondrial biogenesis (low PGC-1α — Tomas 2017)
- Shift to anaerobic metabolism / [[Warburg Effect]] (Naviaux 2016 — "hibernating animal" metabolomic profile)
- Lower VO2 max + worse on 2-day CPET (Keller 2014)
- **2024 muscle biopsy study (LC)**: reduced SDH activity post-PEM; CD68+/CD3+ infiltration; TCA cycle metabolite depletion

### 2. [[Dysautonomia]] / blood flow
- Reduced cerebral blood flow during PEM (Novak 2018 MRI)
- Insufficient blood flow to brain and muscles after exertion

### 3. Immune activation / [[Neuroinflammation]]
- [[Neuroinflammation]] documented via PET (Nakatomi 2014)
- Microglial activation
- Persistent immune response in LC

### 4. [[Viral Persistence and Reactivation]]
- Reactivated [[EBV]] / [[HHV-6]] flare on exertion
- Persistent spike protein (Swank 2022)

## Per [[Dr Gallandi]] (interview synthesis)

Relative tissue ischemia → Krebs cycle runs in reverse on exertion → mitochondrial oxidative stress + lactic acid buildup → delayed fatigue (PEM). Pre-dose [[Oxaloacetate]] before exertion to inhibit SDH; post-exertion use PQQ, hydrogen water, [[Red Light Therapy]], nattokinase ([[Microclots]]).
