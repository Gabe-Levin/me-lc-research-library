# Microclots

Micrometer-scale fibrinaloid clots theorized to impair tissue oxygenation in [[Long COVID]] and possibly [[ME-CFS]]. Trapped inflammatory molecules and amyloid-like fibrin resist normal fibrinolysis. Major framework popularized by Pretorius / Kell.

## Implicated in

- Tissue hypoxia → drives [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]] / [[Warburg Effect]]
- Endothelial dysfunction
- [[Post-Exertional Malaise]] — relative ischemia at exertion → Krebs runs in reverse

## Treatments (per [[Dr Gallandi]] / source synthesis)

- **Nattokinase** — fibrinolytic; "break down microclots"
- **Aspirin** — antiplatelet
- **Vinpocetine** (from periwinkle) — improves cerebral/pulmonary blood flow
- **Ginkgo biloba** — circulation
- **Pycnogenol** — endothelial function
- **Saffron** — re-perfusion (per [[Dr Malcolm]])
- **L-arginine / L-citrulline** — NO donors
- Triple anticoagulant therapy (Pretorius protocol) — TBD in source

## Endothelial support supplements

Lumbro/nattokinase · Glycocalyx Pro / Arterosil HP (Calroy) — glycocalyx repair
