# CMV — Cytomegalovirus (HHV-5)

Lifelong latent herpesvirus. Reactivation implicated in some [[ME-CFS]] cases.

## Diagnostics

- CMV IgG / IgM
- PCR

## Treatment

[[Valganciclovir]] is the antiviral of choice but has more adverse effects than [[Valacyclovir]] / [[Acyclovir]]. Used by Montoya/Lerner in CMV+ cases.
