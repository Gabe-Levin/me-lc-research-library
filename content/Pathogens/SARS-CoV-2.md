# SARS-CoV-2

The virus causing acute COVID-19; persistent SARS-CoV-2 (or its fragments) is one of the leading explanations for [[Long COVID]].

## Persistence evidence

- **Swank et al. 2022, Nature Communications** — persistent SARS-CoV-2 spike protein in LC patients
- **CD16+ monocytes** found to harbor S1 subunit up to 15 months post-infection — basis for [[Maraviroc]] + pravastatin protocol (Patterson)
- Mitochondrial structural damage may persist after viral clearance

## In muscle tissue

- 2024 LC muscle biopsy study: nucleocapsid detected but **not statistically different from controls** — persistence may not be direct cause of [[Post-Exertional Malaise]]

## Reactivates other pathogens

LC patients show elevated incidence of [[EBV]] reactivation (Gold 2021: 66.7% LC vs 10% controls), often early post-infection.

## Acute COVID antivirals being repurposed for LC

- [[Paxlovid]] (nirmatrelvir-ritonavir) — RECOVER STOP-PASC negative
- [[Remdesivir]] — Chia: 75% improvement in ME/CFS subset; LC trial ongoing
- [[Maraviroc]] + pravastatin — Patterson case series
- [[Truvada]] — being studied for LC
