# HHV-6 — Human Herpesvirus 6

Causes roseola in childhood; lifelong latent. Reactivation implicated in [[ME-CFS]]. Can be co-active with [[EBV]] in many ME patients ([[Dr Lerner]] cohorts).

## Diagnostics

- HHV-6 IgG and IgM
- PCR

## Studies

- **Montoya 2012 (n=30)**: ME/CFS patients with high EBV + HHV-6 IgG titers (HHV-6 ≥ 1:320–1:640) treated 6 months [[Valganciclovir]] → many improved (cognitive function, mental fatigue)
- **Bjornevik 2024 review**: persistent HHV-6A/B, HHV-7, B19V more common in ME/CFS

## Ongoing research

Proposed mechanism: characteristic remit/relapse pattern may reflect repeated HHV-6B reactivation in susceptible individuals; pilot saliva study showed higher HHV-6B DNA correlating with symptom severity.

## Treatments

[[Valganciclovir]] (more adverse effects) · [[Valacyclovir]] · [[Acyclovir]] · [[Famciclovir]] · [[IVIG SCIG]]
