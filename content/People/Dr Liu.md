# Dr. Liu

[[Stellate Ganglion Block]] practitioner and researcher. Multiple published case series + cohort studies in [[Long COVID]] and [[ME-CFS]].

## Studies

- 2021 case series (n=2 LC): first SGB report
- Retrospective n=27 (12 analyzed at 1mo): durable responders; PEM, dizziness, tachycardia all reduced
- 2025 [[ME-CFS]] SGB pilot (n=9): post-COVID ME only, mixed results

## Key clinical view

- ME is more neuroinflammatory than immunological
- Pre-COVID ME more entrenched, harder to treat
- Longer disease duration → poorer SGB response
