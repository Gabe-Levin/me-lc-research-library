# Dr. Leo Galland (Gallandi)

Integrative medicine physician. Source interview with [[Dr Putrino]] and [[Dr Malcolm]] frames much of the [[Post-Exertional Malaise]] mechanism + practical protocol in the source document.

## PEM mechanism framing

- Relative tissue ischemia → Krebs cycle runs in reverse during exertion
- Generates oxidative stress + lactic acid → delayed fatigue = [[Post-Exertional Malaise]]
- SDH (Complex II) is the most important reversal enzyme
- **Pre-exertion**: [[Oxaloacetate]] + Chrysin (both inhibit SDH)
- **Post-exertion**: PQQ (related to CoQ10), hydrogen water (Ion Bottle), [[Red Light Therapy]]
- **[[Microclots]]**: Nattokinase, vinpocetine, ginkgo, aspirin, pycnogenol

## Notes

- Some patients don't tolerate OAA (GI side effects)
