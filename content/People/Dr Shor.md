# Dr. Samuel Shor

Internist proposing very high prevalence of [[Lyme Bartonella Babesia]] in [[ME-CFS]]. Estimates up to 88% of his ME/CFS cohort have Lyme+, evidenced by treatment response (often despite seronegative standard CDC tests).

## Caveat

Retrospective with substantial selection + confirmation bias; whether *Borrelia* truly persists in humans is unsettled.
