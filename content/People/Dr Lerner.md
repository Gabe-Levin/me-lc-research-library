# Dr. Lerner

Cardiologist; pioneer of [[Valacyclovir]] / [[EBV]] antiviral protocol for [[ME-CFS]]. Four studies 2002–2010 (n=19, 11, 27, 106) with similarly positive results on energy + ECG abnormalities.

## Protocol

- Valacyclovir 1g q6h (4g/day)
- 6-month minimum; benefits emerge months 5–6
- EIPS energy index tracked; exercise prohibited until EIPS ≥ 7
- ~10–25% need ongoing maintenance
- Continued ≥7 years safely
