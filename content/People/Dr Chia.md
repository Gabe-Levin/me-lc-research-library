# Dr. John Chia

Infectious disease physician. Leading the [[Enteroviruses]] hypothesis for [[ME-CFS]]. Started after his son developed and recovered from ME/CFS. Decades of work; evmedresearch.com.

## Key contributions

- Stomach biopsy series: 82% EV+ in ME/CFS vs 20% controls (Chia 2008)
- Proposes non-cytolytic enterovirus mutants persist long-term
- Brain autopsy: EV detected in deceased ME/CFS patient (Chia 2015)
- Champions [[Oxymatrine Equilibrant]] as first-line; ~50% response rate
- 2021: [[Dihydroquercetin]] efficacy vs CVB4; POTS resolution within month in some patients
- 2011: rifampin augmentation of oxm/equi
- 2025: [[Remdesivir]] 75% remission rate (n=20) in EV+ ME/CFS; [[Paxlovid]] 52% response (n=200)
- ARUP labs Coxsackievirus + Echovirus neutralizing antibody panels are diagnostic standard he recommends
