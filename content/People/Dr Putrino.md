# Dr. David Putrino (PhD)

Leading IACC researcher at Mt. Sinai Center for Complex Chronic Diseases. Runs major [[Long COVID]] trials including:

- **Low-dose [[Low Dose Rapamycin]]** RCT — completion mid-2026
- **[[Truvada]] + [[Maraviroc]]** antiviral trial — completion early 2026

## Key framings

- Even "simple" LC cases (single COVID → ME/CFS-like) have multiple pathogens at presentation: reactivated HHVs, vector-borne ([[Lyme Bartonella Babesia]]) — pathobiology publication expected 2025
- Believes "long Lyme" is an under-researched IACC like ME/CFS
- "When pathogens are reactivating, immunomodulators become important"
- Recommends mast cell stabilizers (curcumin, H1/H2 blockers) for [[MCAS]] overlap
- Frequent menstrual abnormalities → use hormonal therapies (mast cell + histamine link)
- Supplemental oxygen helpful for some ME/CFS patients
