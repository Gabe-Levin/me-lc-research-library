# Dr. Ron Davis (PhD)

Stanford ME/CFS scientist. Director of the Open Medicine Foundation Scientific Advisory Board; runs the Stanford Genome Technology Center. His son has severe ME/CFS, which drives much of his focus.

## Key positions

- Supports more research on [[CIRS]] / [[Mold Mycotoxins]] in [[ME-CFS]]
- Major proponent of basic-science biomarker discovery for ME/CFS subtypes
