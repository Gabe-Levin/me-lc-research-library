# Dr. Maureen Hanson (PhD)

Cornell immunologist. Critical 2021 review of the [[Enteroviruses]] hypothesis in [[ME-CFS]].

## Key points

- Many prior negative EV studies used inadequate detection methods
- 2 ways enteroviruses could still cause ME/CFS: (1) uncommon variant; (2) uncommon reaction to common infection
- Open question whether [[EBV]] causes ME/CFS or merely exploits prior unidentified EV-driven immune disruption
- CVB1 infection of PANC-1 cells downregulates mitochondrial proteins → mechanism link to [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]]

## Papers
- 2021: Critical review of enterovirus theory
- 2023: "The viral origin of myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome"
