# Fibromyalgia

Chronic widespread pain syndrome with significant overlap with [[ME-CFS]]. Studied here primarily because treatments developed for FM frequently get tried in ME/LC.

## Overlapping findings with ME/CFS

- ~76% of severe-end ME/CFS cohort (Bragee 2020) had concurrent FM diagnosis
- 96% allodynia in that cohort
- Shared [[Neuroinflammation]] and [[Oxidative Stress]] markers
- Both respond (modestly) to [[Low Dose Naltrexone]]

## Treatments with FM evidence relevant to ME/LC

- [[HBOT]] — meta-analysis of 9 studies (n=288) shows benefit on tender points, fatigue, sleep, function
- [[Low Dose Naltrexone]] — Younger 2013 RCT (n=30) showed 30% pain reduction vs 18% placebo
- SNRIs (venlafaxine — small open-label trial showed improvement in pain + fatigue + depression)
