# Long COVID

Chronic condition occurring after [[SARS-CoV-2]] infection, present ≥3 months. One of the [[IACCs]].

## Scope

- 200+ symptoms documented
- Multiple presentations: respiratory, neurological, ME/CFS-like
- **≥50% of LC patients meet [[ME-CFS]] criteria** (i.e., they have [[Post-Exertional Malaise]])
- Pathophysiology and prognosis may differ from non-COVID ME/CFS — open question

## Higher-risk populations (per CDC)

Women · Hispanic/Latino · severe acute COVID-19 (esp. ICU) · underlying conditions · age ≥65 · unvaccinated.

## Mechanisms implicated

- [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]] (muscle abnormalities worsen after PEM induction — 2024 study)
- [[Viral Persistence and Reactivation]] — persistent spike protein (Swank et al. 2022); reactivated [[EBV]], [[HHV-6]]
- [[Neuroinflammation]] — persistent immune activation
- [[Microclots]] — endothelial dysfunction
- [[Autonomic Autoantibodies]] (β1, β2, M3, M4) → drives [[POTS]] subset
- [[HPA Axis Dysfunction]] — low cortisol documented

## Treatments studied (LC-specific)

[[HBOT]] · [[NAD Plus]] + [[Low Dose Naltrexone]] (Isman et al. 2024) · [[Low Dose Naltrexone]] alone · [[Stellate Ganglion Block]] (Liu cases; Carroll et al. 86%) · [[Stem Cells]] (Hope Biosciences) · [[Maraviroc]] + pravastatin (Patterson) · [[Truvada]] · [[Paxlovid]] (RECOVER STOP-PASC: negative) · [[Ampligen]] · [[Immunoadsorption]] · [[Remdesivir]] · [[Acyclovir]] · IMC-2 (valacyclovir + celecoxib) · Vidofludimus calcium
