# Dysautonomia

Umbrella term for autonomic nervous system dysfunction. [[POTS]] is the most common form in [[ME-CFS]] / [[Long COVID]].

Orthostatic intolerance is one of the diagnostic criteria for [[ME-CFS]] (when present).
