# Craniocervical Instability (CCI)

Includes CCI, AAI (atlanto-axial instability), and Chiari malformation. Overrepresented in [[ME-CFS]] — particularly in patients with [[EDS Hypermobility]].

## Key findings

**Bragee et al. 2020** (n=229 ME/CFS, severe end of spectrum):
- 50% hypermobile, 20% hEDS
- 55% increased optic nerve sheath diameter
- 83% signs of possible intracranial hypertension; 32% with values indicating more severe IH
- **80% had craniocervical obstructions** on cervical MRI (mostly disc bulges/hernias)
- 96% allodynia (harmless pressure → pain)
- 76% [[Fibromyalgia]] co-diagnosed

**Johns Hopkins case series**: 3 ME/CFS patients with cervical spinal stenosis had marked symptom improvement after decompressive surgery.

**Jen Brea**: documented (temporary?) remission after CCI surgery.

## Treatment

Surgical (decompression, fusion) — high-risk, last-resort. Conservative: PT, supportive collars, postural awareness.
