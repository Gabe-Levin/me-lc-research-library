# MCAS — Mast Cell Activation Syndrome

Mast cells with lowered activation threshold and/or excessive mediator release. Common comorbidity of [[ME-CFS]] and [[Long COVID]] — treating MCAS may reduce overall symptom burden.

## Treatments (per source synthesis with [[Dr Putrino]], [[Dr Gallandi]], [[Dr Malcolm]])

- H1 + H2 blockers (cetirizine/loratadine/fexofenadine + famotidine)
- Mast cell stabilizers: cromolyn, ketotifen (compounded, sedating), quercetin
- LTRAs: montelukast (Singulair)
- Other: luteolin (Mirica), DAO enzyme with meals, amlexanox (compounded, for mouth ulcers)
- Statins (vasculature + maybe mast cells/endothelium)
- Curcumin, prickly pear
- [[Low Dose Naltrexone]] — also modulates mast cells via TLR4
