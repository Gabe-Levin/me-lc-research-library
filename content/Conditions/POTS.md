# POTS — Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome

A form of [[Dysautonomia]] characterized by excessive heart rate increase on standing. Common comorbidity of [[ME-CFS]] and [[Long COVID]].

## Symptoms

Lightheadedness, palpitations, exercise intolerance, brain fog, fatigue worse upright, near-syncope.

## Mechanism implicated in post-viral POTS

- **[[Autonomic Autoantibodies]]** — β1, β2 adrenergic and M3/M4 muscarinic; basis for [[Immunoadsorption]] and [[IVIG SCIG]] trials
- Small fiber neuropathy (subset)
- Hypovolemia / endothelial dysfunction

## Treatments (per source)

### Medications
- Ivabradine (HR control) — can prescribe from Canadian pharmacy if uncovered
- Guanfacine (helps brain fog too)
- Stimulants (BP support in orthostatic hypotension)
- Fludrocortisone (volume — needs potassium support)
- Midodrine

### Lifestyle
- Salt + electrolytes (WHO ORS protocol; LMNT, etc.)
- Compression
- HOB elevation 20–30°
- High-protein/fat, smaller meals
- Recumbent exercise (Levine, CHOP, UTAH ADaPT protocols) — **only if PEM-aware**

### Supplements
- Licorice root (BP)
- Glycocalyx Pro / Arterosil HP (endothelium)
- Nattokinase (circulation)

### Advanced
- [[IVIG SCIG]] — RECOVER-AUTO trial, plus several case series for autoantibody-positive POTS
- [[Stellate Ganglion Block]] — autonomic reset
- Vagus nerve stimulation (TBD)
