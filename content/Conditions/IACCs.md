# IACCs — Infection-Associated Chronic Conditions

Umbrella term for chronic illnesses triggered by infection. Includes [[ME-CFS]], [[Long COVID]], post-treatment [[Lyme Bartonella Babesia]] syndrome (PTLDS), and arguably [[Fibromyalgia]].

## Clinical reference for HCPs

Concise CME-bearing clinical review: Mayo Clinic Proceedings 2023 — https://www.mayoclinicproceedings.org/article/S0025-6196(23)00402-0/fulltext

## Shared features across IACCs

- Triggered by infection (or sometimes reactivation of latent infection — see [[Viral Persistence and Reactivation]])
- [[Post-Exertional Malaise]] is common (defining for [[ME-CFS]])
- Multi-system involvement (energy, cognition, autonomic, immune)
- Female-predominant in most cohorts
- Frequent comorbidity stack: [[POTS]], [[MCAS]], [[EDS Hypermobility]], [[Craniocervical Instability]]
- Often involves multiple pathogens by the time of presentation — see [[Dr Putrino]] pathobiology framing
