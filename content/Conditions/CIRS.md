# CIRS — Chronic Inflammatory Response Syndrome

"The mold hypothesis." Not a formally medically accepted diagnosis. Severe inflammatory response to mold exposure, proposed by [[Dr Shoemaker]] et al. Some clinicians believe a subset of [[ME-CFS]] is CIRS at root; others see mold sensitivity as downstream of immune dysfunction.

## Evidence base

- Brewer et al. 2013 (n=112 CFS): **93% positive for urinary mycotoxins**, 90% had water-damaged building exposure. Criticism: control group was specifically chosen as non-WDB-exposed.
- Aspergillus mycotoxins prevalence study (n=236 ME/CFS): 92.4% had Ochratoxin A. Criticism: no control, no general-population prevalence data.
- Tim Henrich's UCSF lab: mold detected in ~⅓ of Long COVID patients.
- [[Dr Davis]] (Stanford): supports need for more research.

## Tests (controversial / not FDA-approved)

- Urine mycotoxin panel
- MMP-9 (elevated >350 may indicate)
- VEGF (suppressed <31 may indicate)
- C4a
- ERMI Swiffer dust test (for the environment)
