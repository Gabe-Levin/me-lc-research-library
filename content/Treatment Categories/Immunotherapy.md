# Immunotherapy

Category note for immune-modulating treatments used in [[ME-CFS]] / [[Long COVID]].

## Modalities

- [[IVIG SCIG]] — antibody-replacement / immunomodulation
- [[Immunoadsorption]] — removes IgG including pathogenic [[Autonomic Autoantibodies]]
- [[Low Dose Naltrexone]] — TLR4 antagonism, microglial modulation, mast cell effect
- [[Low Dose Rapamycin]] — mTOR inhibition; immune + microglial + antiviral
- [[Stem Cells]] — Hope Biosciences trials (LC)
- Hydroxychloroquine (Plaquenil) — autoimmunity overlap
- Monoclonal antibodies — early/experimental in LC
- [[Ampligen]] — TLR-3 agonist; immunomodulatory + antiviral
- Plasmapheresis (case report follow-up to IA)
