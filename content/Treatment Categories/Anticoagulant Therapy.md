# Anticoagulant Therapy

Targets [[Microclots]] and endothelial dysfunction in [[Long COVID]] / [[ME-CFS]].

## Common agents (per source)

- Nattokinase (fibrinolytic)
- Aspirin (antiplatelet)
- Vinpocetine (cerebral/pulmonary blood flow)
- Ginkgo biloba (circulation)
- Pycnogenol (endothelial)
- Saffron (re-perfusion — [[Dr Malcolm]])
- Triple anticoagulant therapy (Pretorius protocol) — TBD in source

## Supportive endothelial supplements

L-arginine · L-citrulline · Glycocalyx Pro / Arterosil HP (glycocalyx repair)
