# Ampligen (Rintatolimod)

Double-stranded RNA TLR-3 agonist. One of the only drugs developed specifically for [[ME-CFS]]. Long-running development with denied 2012 FDA approval; resurfacing for [[Long COVID]].

## Mechanism

Antiviral + immunomodulatory — increases antiviral immune response.

## Studies — [[ME-CFS]]

- **Strayer 1995** (n=15): improvements in cognition + memory; HHV6 mRNA reduction; long-term observation
- **Strayer 2012 RCT** (n=234, severe ME): IV BIW × 40 weeks → modest improvements in exercise tolerance; FDA panel voted 8-5 against approval in Dec 2012. Compassionate care program followed (≤100 patients)
- **AIM 2016 reanalysis**: in 2–8 year disease duration subgroup, 51% Ampligen vs 18% placebo had ≥25% improvement in exercise tolerance — disease duration matters

## Studies — [[Long COVID]]

- **AIM Phase II 2023–2024** (n=80; 66 completed): 13 weeks — primary PROMIS Fatigue endpoint NS at week 13. But Ampligen outperformed placebo in 12/13 tracked weeks; 6MWT improvements significant in baseline <205m subset (139m vs 91m). AIM advocates future trials focusing on moderate-or-worse LC fatigue.

## Practical

- Very expensive
- IV BIW
- Need more trials to clarify subset that responds
