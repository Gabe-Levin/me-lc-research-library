# Red Light Therapy / Photobiomodulation (PBM)

Low-risk adjunct. Wavelengths 600–700 nm (red) + 750–1500 nm (NIR-A) penetrate tissue and modulate mitochondrial activity.

## Mechanisms

- Enhances cytochrome c oxidase (Complex IV) activity → boosts ATP
- Reduces inflammation (NF-κB ↓, NF-κB-dependent gene expression ↓; M1 macrophage markers ↓)
- Increases melatonin production in mitochondria
- Activates heat shock proteins → autophagy
- Synergizes with [[Methylene Blue]] (same axes)

## Wavelength penetration

NIR-A deepest tissue penetration (up to 23 cm). NIR-A 1000–1500 nm optimal for tissue heating.

## Treatable conditions emerging in literature

Stroke, TBI, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, depression — transcranial PBM.

- **Pereira 2023** — sham-controlled study of IR LED for hospitalized COVID-19 → marked improvement
- Studies for [[Long COVID]] / [[ME-CFS]] specifically still emerging

## Options

- Red light panel (Sarah uses small EMR-Tek $200 — 630/670/850 nm)
- Infrared sauna
