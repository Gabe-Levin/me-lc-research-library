# Oxaloacetate (OAA)

A Krebs cycle intermediate. Proposed treatment for [[ME-CFS]] / [[Long COVID]] energy dysfunction. Promising, expensive, GI side effects common.

## Mechanism

- Direct Krebs intermediate
- **Inhibits SDH** (Complex II / succinate dehydrogenase) — important because in PEM the Krebs cycle runs in reverse, and SDH is the most important reversal enzyme. OAA blocks the reverse-running ([[Dr Gallandi]] framing). Chrysin is another SDH inhibitor.
- **Signaling molecule** that controls the [[Warburg Effect]] (cytoplasmic glycolysis shift) — recently described
- Roles in gluconeogenesis, fatty acid synthesis, amino acid synthesis, glyoxylate cycle, urea cycle, lactate cycle

## Studies

**RESTORE ME RCT** — n=82 ME/CFS, 2,000 mg/day × 90 days vs placebo. Fatigue ↓ 27% (treatment) vs 10% (placebo). ~½ of treatment arm had "enhanced response."

## Practical

- Pre-dose before exertion ([[Dr Gallandi]])
- GI side effects in a subset
- Brand: benaGene (anhydrous enol-OAA) is the common one
- AM dosing only (some report insomnia)
- ~€80–200/mo
