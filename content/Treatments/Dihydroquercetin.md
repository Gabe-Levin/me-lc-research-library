# Dihydroquercetin (Taxifolin)

Flavonoid from larch, Douglas fir, milk thistle. Adds two hydrogens to quercetin → stronger antioxidant per preclinical work.

## Use case

[[Dr Chia]] (2021): demonstrated by Russian groups against CVB4. In some [[ME-CFS]] patients with chronic [[Enteroviruses]]:
- POTS resolved within a month
- Mast cell stabilization

## Practical

Anti-inflammatory + antioxidant; broad-spectrum supportive use across [[Oxidative Stress]] and [[MCAS]] overlap.
