# Remdesivir

Broad-spectrum RNA virus antiviral. FDA-approved for acute COVID IV (oral version emerging). Being trialed in [[Long COVID]] (completion late 2025).

## Chia 2025 — ME/CFS with chronic [[Enteroviruses]]

**75% (15/20)** achieved remission of [[ME-CFS]] after 5–10 days of IV remdesivir ± immune modulators.
- Hospitalized COVID + ME/CFS: 10/12 responded
- Non-COVID ME/CFS: 5/8 responded
- Remission lasted 6–8 weeks to 6–9 months before relapse
- Controls (n=20, no remdesivir): 2/20 mild improvement (p < 0.001)
- EV protein in PBL decreased with response, increased with relapse

## Rationale

RNA antiviral active vs both SARS-CoV-2 and enteroviruses — strong mechanistic case for the EV-driven ME subset.

## Chia 2021 observation

ME/FM patients hospitalized with acute COVID who received remdesivir reported **improvement of their underlying ME/FM** — one became "normal" for 1 month after 8 years of illness.
