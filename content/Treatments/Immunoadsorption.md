# Immunoadsorption (IA)

Apheresis-like procedure that removes IgG antibodies from plasma. Primary use in post-COVID [[ME-CFS]] / [[POTS]] subset with elevated [[Autonomic Autoantibodies]] (β1, β2, M3, M4). Mostly Germany-centric clinics.

## Mechanism / rationale

Removes pathogenic autoantibodies, particularly anti-β1/β2 adrenergic and anti-M3/M4 muscarinic. Theory: these autoantibodies misfire ANS receptors → POTS/orthostatic symptoms + symptom severity correlation.

## Studies

### Schulze et al. 2025 (n=12 post-COVID ME/CFS, elevated ANSR Abs)
- 5 IA sessions over 10 days
- ↓ pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-4, IL-2, IL-1β, TNF, IL-17A)
- ↓ soluble spike protein
- Objective improvements in neuropsych + hand grip
- **NS improvement on SF-36, Chalder, Bell, or Canadian criteria self-report**
- Antibody rebound within ~1 month

### Sotzny 2024 (n=20 post-COVID ME/CFS with elevated β2)
- 5 IA sessions
- 70% responders (≥10 point SF-36 PF gain)
- Peak benefit months 2–3; some lasting through month 6
- 7/20 received a 2nd cycle for relapse

### IA-PACS-CFS RCT interim Oct 2023 (n=10)
- 7/10 ≥10pt SF-36 PF at week 4

### Scheibenbogen 2018 + 2020 (n=10 + 5 follow-up ME)
- 7/10 improved after IA1; 4/5 improved after IA2
- Endothelial dysfunction also improved
- 1 case: plasmapheresis at 9 months post-IA2 nearly resolved symptoms

## Ongoing trials

- NCT05710770: n=66, completion Feb 2025
- NCT05954325: n=60, completion June 2025

## Notes

Larger Phase III data may move this into mainstream subgroup-targeted use. Need replication.
