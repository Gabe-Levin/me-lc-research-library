# Methylene Blue (MB)

A century-old dye with multiple mechanisms. May "patch" failing mitochondrial energy production while addressing antimicrobial and neuroinflammatory axes.

## Mechanisms

- **Electron carrier**: bypasses Complex I leakage → reroutes electrons to Complex III → less ROS, more ATP at low dose (LDMB)
- **BBB crossing**: high brain uptake; CNS levels 10× serum
- **Induces mitophagy** (autophagy of damaged mitochondria)
- **Anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, neuroprotective**
- **Antimicrobial / antiviral** — used historically for malaria, UTIs, methaemoglobinaemia; recent in vitro activity vs SARS-CoV-2 and H1N1 without UV activation
- **Synergistic with [[Red Light Therapy]]** / [[Photobiomodulation]] — same axes (mitochondrial, anti-inflammatory)

## Dose

- Oral: 10–30 mg daily (lowest effective)
- Higher 50–100 mg BID for antimicrobial intent
- IV better penetrates nervous system (per 1 study)
- COVID studies used 2–3 mg/kg

## Warnings

- **MUST get G6PD test first** (hemolysis risk in deficient patients)
- Many interactions: SSRIs/MAOIs (serotonin syndrome)
- Source from a *legitimate compounding pharmacy* (CFS pharmacy)
- Monitor haptoglobin periodically

## Studies / proposed roles in COVID

- 4 papers exploring antiviral and inflammation-modulating roles in acute and post-COVID neurocognitive impairment
- Inhibits excess NO synthesis
- All low-quality evidence
