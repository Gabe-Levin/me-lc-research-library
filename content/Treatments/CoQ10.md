# CoQ10 (Ubiquinone / Ubiquinol)

Electron carrier in the mitochondrial ETC; depleted in [[ME-CFS]] and [[Long COVID]]. Ubiquinol = reduced/active form, better bioavailability than ubiquinone.

## Studies

- **Castro-Marrero 2021** (n=207 ME/CFS, RCT) — CoQ10 200mg + NADH 20mg × 8 weeks → modest improvements in fatigue, QoL, sleep
- LC clinical trials ongoing (per source)

## Practical

- Target dose 200–400 mg/day for ubiquinol
- Often paired with [[NAD Plus]] (NADH or NR)
- Included in [[Phosphatidylcholine]]-based products (ATP360, ATP Fuel)
- 4-week washout needed before mitochondrial diagnostic panels (may obscure deficiency)
