# IVIG / SCIG

Intravenous / subcutaneous immunoglobulin. Modulates immune response via multiple mechanisms: neutralizes autoantibodies and cytokines, saturates FcRn, inhibits complement, regulates T/B cells.

## Rationale in [[ME-CFS]] / [[Long COVID]] / [[POTS]]

Targets [[Autonomic Autoantibodies]], post-infectious autoimmunity, immune dysregulation. Best-studied in autoantibody-positive [[POTS]] subset.

## Relevant testing (insurance gate)

Strep pneumo antibodies (pneumovax challenge), IgG total + subclasses 1–4, MBL + complement function, IgM/IgA/IgE. Low total IgG/IgM → CVID. Low subclass 1 or 2 → SAD qualifies for IVIG. Workup by immunologist.

## Studies

### [[ME-CFS]]
- **Brenu 2024** (n=17, low-dose SCIG 0.06 g/kg/mo × 5 wks): significant improvements in symptoms, QoL, work capacity; 7 had QoL +35 (out of 100); 1 complete remission
- **Devanur 2021 review**: case for reviving IgG approach; supports trial in severe ME/CFS with viral-like onset
- **Italian 2013** (n=741): antiviral/Ig group did best across treatment categories (15% positive vs 8% negative response)

### [[Long COVID]]
- **Thompson 2022** (n=9): long-term high-dose Ig → significant clinical benefit in 6 treated patients
- **Khan 2025 review**: emerging support in PASC, mixed ME/CFS evidence

### [[POTS]]
- **Schofield 2022** (n=7 SCIG/PLEX): ~50% COMPASS-31 ↓, 217% FAS ↑; 6/7 reduced/discontinued oral POTS meds
- **iSTAND RCT 2024** (n=30): IVIG 47% vs albumin 38% responder rate — NS
- **Weinstock 2021** (n=6): ~40% symptom severity ↓ after 6 months
- **N=1 SCIG case** (anti-β1/2 + M2/4 + nociceptin antibodies): SFN-confirmed neuropathic POTS responds; SCIG better tolerated than IVIG

## Ongoing trials

- **RECOVER-AUTONOMIC** Appendix A: IVIG for LC autonomic dysfunction; recruiting; completion ~end 2025
- **IgPro20 in post-COVID POTS** double-blind RCT: SCIG; completion 2027
