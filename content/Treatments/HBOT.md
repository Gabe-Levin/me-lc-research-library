# HBOT — Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy

Pressurized oxygen breathing. Promising data in [[Long COVID]], [[ME-CFS]], and [[Fibromyalgia]] — but expensive (sessions ~$150–500), and often needs 40–60+ sessions for durable effect, especially for longer-duration illness.

## Mechanisms

- Anti-inflammatory: ↓ pro-inflammatory cytokines, ↑ IL-10
- Polarizes macrophages → M2 phenotype
- Improves mitochondrial function ([[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]])
- Stimulates angiogenesis
- Antimicrobial at high pressures (2.0–2.4 ATA)
- Surprisingly the pressure itself (not just O2) mediates much of the effect

## Standards

- High-pressure (2.0–2.4 ATA) with 100% O2 + 60–90 min sessions = max response
- Lower pressure (<1.5 ATA) without O2 = present but smaller effect, more sessions needed
- ≥40 sessions standard; 10-session trial sufficient to determine therapeutic failure

## Studies

### [[Long COVID]]
- **Zilberman-Itskovich 2022 RCT** (n=73, 40 sessions, 2 ATA): significant cognitive, energy, sleep, psychiatric, pain improvements; MRI brain perfusion + microstructure changes
- **Hadanny 2024**: 1-year follow-up of above — durable across domains *except* physical functioning and sleep. But cohort had only 5–6mo of illness.
- **Robbins 2021** (n=10, 10 sessions 2.4 ATA): Chalder fatigue, cognition, executive function, attention — all large effect sizes

### [[ME-CFS]]
- **Akarsu 2013** (n=16, no controls, 15 sessions 2.4 ATA): good QoL/fatigue improvements; no long-term f/u
- **Van Hoof 2003** (n=29, 1 week with mycoplasma split): negative — likely under-dosed

### [[Fibromyalgia]]
- 2023 meta-analysis (9 studies, n=288): significant improvement in symptoms, tender points; AE rate 23.8%; ↓ pressure (<2.0 ATA) safer

## Caveats

- **May be contraindicated for latent herpesviruses** ([[EBV]], [[HHV-6]]) — oxidative stress can promote latent cell expansion
- Aviv Clinic FL: $50–60k comprehensive program (12–13 weeks)
- Not every chamber is the same; must be medical-grade

## Per I-Recover protocol

If no clinical benefit after 10 sessions → therapeutic failure. Continue until plateau, then taper.
