# Valacyclovir

Guanosine analogue. Metabolized to acyclovir in the body. Most commonly used antiviral for [[EBV]] reactivation in [[ME-CFS]]. Champion: [[Dr Lerner]].

## Lerner protocol

- 1g q6h (= 4g/day)
- Herxheimer-style worsening for 2–6 weeks early on = good prognostic sign
- Improvements emerge at months 5–6
- Once EIPS ≥ 7 reached, allow light exercise
- Maintenance: 1g BID × 6–12 weeks, then stop
- ~20% need ongoing maintenance to prevent relapse
- Continued ≥7 years in some patients without ill effects

## Studies

- **Lerner et al. 4 studies (2002–2010)**: n=19, 11, 27, 106 — similarly positive on energy + ECG improvements
- **Stanford 2007** (n=27): significant improvement in physical activity in EBV+ ME — but treatment changes muddied interpretation
- **IMC-2 / Dogwood 2024** LC interim: valacyclovir 750mg + celecoxib 200mg BID showed clinically meaningful fatigue + sleep improvements vs placebo
- **Acyclovir case series 2023**: 4 LC patients with neuro symptoms resolved + IgM/IgG reduction

## Other notes

- See [[Dr Myhill]] writeup of Lerner protocol
- Acyclovir 1988 trial (n=24) was negative — Lerner explains by under-dosing / wrong cohort
