# NAD+ / NADH

Critical mitochondrial cofactor. Depletes in aging, obesity, [[SARS-CoV-2]] infection, and chronic inflammation. Targeting NAD+ may improve mitochondrial bioenergetics and reduce inflammatory cytokines.

## Options (cheapest → most expensive)

### NAD+ precursors (boost endogenous NAD+)
- **NR (nicotinamide riboside)** — Tru Niagen 300–500 mg AM. Best-studied. Cell uptake well-characterized.
- **NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide)** — uptake less clear
- **Oral NADH** — Greenfood etc., 10–20 mg daily
- (Avoid NA / NAM: flushing, methyl depletion, hepatotoxicity)

### Direct NAD+
- Patches (IontoPATCH 400 mg/mL, ~$200/6 wks via Ageless Rx)
- Nasal spray
- Injections, IV (expensive, limited evidence)

## Studies

### [[ME-CFS]]
- **Castro-Marrero 2021** (n=207, RCT): CoQ10 200mg + NADH 20mg × 8 weeks → modest improvements in fatigue, QoL, sleep
- **Forsyth 1999** (n=26): NADH 10mg × 4 weeks → 31% favorable response vs 8% placebo
- **Santaella 2004** (n=31): NADH × 24 months — large first-trimester improvement; gap closed by alternative arm later

### [[Long COVID]]
- **Isman et al. 2024** (n=36 mod/severe LC): [[Low Dose Naltrexone]] + NAD+ patches → SF-36 36→52; ~½ responders (≥20% improvement)

### Mechanism
- Restores mitochondrial bioenergetics
- Reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-2, IL-5, IL-6 in one RCT)
- COVID-mediated NAD+ depletion has multiple proposed sources (CD38↑, viral-induced consumption)

## Practical

- Cheaper to try NR (Niagen) or NADH first vs IV/patches
- IV injections/IVs lack RCT data in ME/LC; small case reports only
- Multiple LC trials in progress for NR
