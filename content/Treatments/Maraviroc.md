# Maraviroc

CCR5 antagonist HIV drug. Repurposed for [[Long COVID]] based on CD16+ monocyte spike persistence theory.

## Rationale (Patterson framework)

S1 spike subunit persists up to 15 months in CD16+ monocytes after acute COVID. These monocytes express both CCR5 and CX3CR1 (fractalkine receptors) and play roles in vascular homeostasis + endothelial surveillance. Block CCR5 (maraviroc) + block fractalkine (pravastatin) → disrupt monocytic-endothelial-platelet axis.

## Studies

- **Patterson case series (n=18)** — Maraviroc 300mg BID + pravastatin 10mg daily × 6–12 weeks. Significant improvement on 5 validated scales; ↓ sCD40L + VEGF vascular markers.

## Trials in progress

- **Putrino Mt. Sinai** LC RCT (with [[Truvada]]) — completion early 2026

## Practical

- Risks: hepatotoxicity (black-box FDA warning) — monitor LFTs
- Limited published data
- Price and availability barrier
