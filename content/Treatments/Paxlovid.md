# Paxlovid (Nirmatrelvir-Ritonavir)

Pfizer's acute COVID antiviral. Trialed for [[Long COVID]].

## RECOVER STOP-PASC RCT (n=155, ≥3 months PASC)

15-day course of nirmatrelvir-ritonavir in mostly vaccinated cohort: **generally safe, did NOT show significant benefit** on fatigue, brain fog, body aches, cardiovascular, dyspnea, GI symptoms.

## Chia 2025 ME/CFS [[Enteroviruses]] study

PAX in N=200 ME/CFS with chronic EV → **52% improved**, often within 2–3 days. But all relapsed days–weeks after stopping. Response by EV subset:
- CVB4+: 66%
- CVB3+: 33%
- Non-CVB3/4+: 44%
