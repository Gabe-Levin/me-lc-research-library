# Stellate Ganglion Block (SGB)

Anesthetic injection into the stellate (sympathetic) ganglion in the neck. Used for PTSD, CRPS. Now being trialed in [[Long COVID]] and [[ME-CFS]] for autonomic reset. Anecdotally polarizing — some near-miraculous recoveries, many non-responders.

## Mechanisms (proposed)

- Restores parasympathetic balance via sympathetic blockade
- Disrupts overactive nerve-immune-inflammation feedback loops
- Downstream: ↓ cytokines, improved endothelium, microcirculation
- For LC: theorized to break sympathetic over-/vagal under-activity cycle perpetuating chronic inflammation

## Studies

### [[Long COVID]]
- **Carroll 2023** (n=41, chronic pain practice): 86% had some symptom relief; fatigue 77%, brain fog 80%, PEM 74%. Note: pre-screened by procedural willingness; "some relief" not quantified
- **Liu retrospective** (n=27, 12 analyzed): 1-month follow-up — 10/12 durable responders. PEM after physical activity 5.7/10 ↓; mental PEM 5.3/10 ↓; orthostatic dizziness 4.8/10 ↓; tachycardia 5.5/10 ↓; anxiety 4.0/10 ↓; depression 4.2/10 ↓
- **Liu case series 2021** (n=2): two LC successes
- **Olfactory dysfunction pilot 2024** (n=20): 50% subjective improvement; 55% UPSIT; 35% ODOR
- **Kuo Mt. Sinai** retrospective in progress: 1000s of SGBs; "most find clinically meaningful improvement"

### [[ME-CFS]]
- **Liu 2025 ME/CFS pilot** (n=9): post-COVID ME only; SGB ×3 over 3 weeks
  - Helps POTS somewhat but not the best treatment
  - Liu hypothesis: ME is more neuroinflammatory than immunological — longer duration = harder to treat
  - Pre-COVID ME more entrenched, more resistant

## Carroll/Liu observation

**Delayed SGB in long-standing post-viral conditions** (Lyme, ME, MECFS) → notably less response — 10–20% reduction per injection — vs LC.

## Practical

- $300–$2,000+ per procedure depending on practitioner
- Rarely insurance-covered (except CRPS, sometimes PTSD)
- Bilateral or sequential (R first, then L after Horner's resolves)
- "Stellate Ganglion Block and ME" FB group — read with caveat (selection bias toward non-responders posting)
