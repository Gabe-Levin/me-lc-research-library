# HPA Axis Dysfunction

Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Adrenal axis dysregulation — typically low cortisol — documented in [[ME-CFS]] and [[Long COVID]]. Proposed to be a consequence of upstream immune alteration rather than a primary lesion.

## Testing

- Morning serum cortisol
- 24h urine cortisol
- DUTCH (dried urine; not FDA-approved, functional medicine context)
- ACTH stimulation test (if frank insufficiency suspected)
- DHEA-S
- Look at free cortisol, not just total

## Treatment

Hormone replacement / support depending on the pattern; not detailed in source. Adaptogens for adrenal support (general supplement section).
