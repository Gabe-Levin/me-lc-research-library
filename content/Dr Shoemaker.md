# Dr. Ritchie Shoemaker

Originator of the [[CIRS]] (chronic inflammatory response syndrome) framework. Proposes a genetic-susceptibility model (HLA-DR typing) for mold/biotoxin-driven chronic illness.

Diagnostic panel typically includes MMP-9, VEGF, C4a, TGF-β1, MSH, VIP, and HLA-DR genotype.
