# ME/CFS

Myalgic Encephalomyelitis / Chronic Fatigue Syndrome. A chronic, multi-system, infection-associated illness. One of the [[IACCs]].

## Diagnosis (IOM 2015 criteria)

**Three required:**
1. ≥6 months of substantial reduction in pre-illness activity, with profound new-onset fatigue not relieved by rest
2. **[[Post-Exertional Malaise]]** — the hallmark
3. **Unrefreshing sleep**

**Plus ≥1 of:**
- Cognitive impairment ("brain fog")
- Orthostatic intolerance (see [[POTS]] / [[Dysautonomia]])

Common but non-required: muscle pain, joint pain, new headaches, tender lymph nodes, sore throat, light/sound sensitivity, food/chemical sensitivity (overlap with [[MCAS]]).

## Scale and overlap

- ~3.3M Americans, possibly 5–9M post-pandemic
- **≥50% of [[Long COVID]] patients meet ME/CFS diagnostic criteria** (i.e., have PEM)
- Common comorbidities: [[POTS]], [[Dysautonomia]], [[MCAS]], [[EDS Hypermobility]], [[Craniocervical Instability]], [[Fibromyalgia]], migraine

## Proposed root causes (subtype hypotheses)

Sarah's "perfect storm" theory: multiple insults converge — viral infection + chronic stress + reactivated [[EBV]] + HPA dysfunction. Subtype candidates:

- **Viral reactivation** ([[EBV]], [[HHV-6]], [[CMV]]) — see [[Dr Lerner]]
- **[[Enteroviruses]]** (Coxsackie, Echovirus) — see [[Dr Chia]], [[Dr Hanson]]
- **Acquired mitochondrial myopathy** — see [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]] (Wirth & Scheibenbogen)
- **[[Autonomic Autoantibodies]]** (β1, β2, M3, M4) — basis for [[Immunoadsorption]] trials
- **[[Lyme Bartonella Babesia]]** (subset; controversial) — see [[Dr Shor]]
- **[[CIRS]] / [[Mold Mycotoxins]]** (subset; controversial)
- **[[Craniocervical Instability]]** (subset) — see Jen Brea remission

We desperately need more research to delineate subtypes.
