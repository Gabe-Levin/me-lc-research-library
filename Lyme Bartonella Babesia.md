# Lyme / Bartonella / Babesia (Lyme+)

Vector-borne infections. Proposed by some clinicians as a root cause for a subset of [[ME-CFS]] / [[Long COVID]]. Standing of "chronic Lyme" / PTLDS (post-treatment Lyme disease syndrome) is contested — see 2025 National Academies report: https://nap.nationalacademies.org/catalog/28578/charting-a-path-toward-new-treatments-for-lyme-infection-associated-chronic-illnesses

## Theory

- *Borrelia* may evade immune detection (intracellular forms; biofilm)
- Persistence demonstrated in primates; not yet in humans
- May "reactivate" when immunity weakens — similar narrative to [[EBV]] but for bacteria
- [[Dr Putrino]]: believes "long Lyme" is under-researched IACC like ME/CFS

## Evidence

- **Shor**: estimates up to 88% of his ME/CFS cohort have Lyme+; many improved with treatment despite seronegative standard tests. Retrospective + biased.
- **Patrick 2015 IACFS abstract**: PTLDS shares pathogenesis with ME/CFS
- **Maggi 2012**: 41% bacteremia for Bartonella in rheumatology referral cohort
- **Multiple LC case studies**: Bartonella henselae reactivation in LC patients; improvement with treatment

## Testing

- **iDart** (FDA-approved, newer)
- **iGeneX** — recommended by Mt. Sinai CORE
- **T-Labs** — RNA detection (not FDA-approved)
- **CD57** — immune marker
- ARUP, LabCorp recommended by CDC but **low sensitivity for late-stage** per Columbia Lyme
- Bartonella: NC State College of Veterinary Medicine has cutting-edge testing (60 Degrees Pharmaceuticals study)

## Caveats

[[Stellate Ganglion Block]] subset: per Liu, long-standing post-viral patients (Lyme, ME, MECFS) show notably diminished SGB response (10–20% relief per injection) vs LC.
