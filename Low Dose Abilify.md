# Low Dose Abilify (Aripiprazole)

Atypical antipsychotic. Used at 0.2–2 mg/day (much lower than antipsychotic dosing) off-label for [[ME-CFS]] fatigue, brain fog, unrefreshing sleep, PEM frequency.

## Mechanism rationale

Dopamine D2 agonist effects → may mediate [[Neuroinflammation]], microglial activation, cell death (animal + human evidence).

## Stanford retrospective 2021 (n=101)

- Mean dose 1.1 mg/day; start 0.25 mg, titrate
- **75/101 (74%) improved** in ≥1 of fatigue / brain fog / unrefreshing sleep / PEM frequency
- 12 (12%) no observable difference
- 14 (14%) worsening or side effects → discontinued
