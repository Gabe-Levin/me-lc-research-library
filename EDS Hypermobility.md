# EDS / Hypermobility

Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (especially hypermobile, hEDS) and Hypermobility Spectrum Disorders (HSD) are overrepresented in [[ME-CFS]].

- hEDS prevalence in general population: <1%
- Prevalence in ME/CFS cohort (Bragee et al. 2020, n=229): **20% formally hEDS**, 50% hypermobile
- Joint hypermobility more common in pediatric ME/CFS than controls

## Why it matters

- Connective tissue laxity → autonomic instability ([[POTS]])
- Cervical instability → [[Craniocervical Instability]] overlap
- Often clustered with [[MCAS]] (the "trifecta": EDS + POTS + MCAS)
