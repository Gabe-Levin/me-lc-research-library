# Phosphatidylcholine (PC) / Phospholipids

Membrane lipid; may repair cellular and mitochondrial membranes damaged by infection or chronic stress. The mitochondrial inner membrane is where energy production happens — restoring it is the proposed therapeutic logic.

## Mechanism

- SARS-CoV-2 directly damages mitochondria; membrane repair may help even after viral clearance (2022 paper)
- Important for [[CIRS]] patients (Shoemaker framework — IV PC + glutathione)

## Options

### IV (most bioavailable)
- Start 5 cc (250 mg) PC, increase by 5 cc/wk to tolerance (max 50 cc / 2,500 mg)
- Optional add-on glutathione 0.5 → 1–2 g
- Series of 10+ sessions
