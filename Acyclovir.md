# Acyclovir

Guanosine analogue antiviral. Predecessor of [[Valacyclovir]] (valacyclovir is metabolized to acyclovir; valacyclovir has better oral bioavailability).

## Studies

- **1988 ME/CFS trial (n=24)** — IV acyclovir 1 week → oral 1 month — NS difference vs control. Likely under-dosed.
- **2023 Acyclovir LC case series (n=4)** — neuro symptoms resolved; IgG/IgM titers reduced.
