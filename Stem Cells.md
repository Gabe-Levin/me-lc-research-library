# Stem Cells

Mesenchymal stem cell infusions. Promising early results in [[Long COVID]]; pivotal Phase II trial pending full publication.

## Hope Biosciences Research Foundation (TX)

### Pilot (n=10)
9/10 had 75–100% improvement; 10th had ~25%. Mostly measured fatigue (VAS) + SF-36. 98% no side effects (mild headache, post-infusion fatigue, flu-like).

### Phase II RCT (n=64, 4 infusions × 200M stem cells)
- 68% treatment improvement (p = .0002)
- **63% placebo improvement (p = .001)**
- Treatment vs placebo: not statistically significant
- Interpretation pending — unexpectedly large placebo response masks treatment effect
