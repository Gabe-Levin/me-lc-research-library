# Mold / Mycotoxins

Exposure to mold (especially from water-damaged buildings, WDB) and associated mycotoxins. Hypothesized cause/contributor for [[CIRS]] subset of [[ME-CFS]].

## Evidence

- **Brewer 2013** (n=112 CFS): 93% positive urine mycotoxins; 90% had WDB exposure. Criticism: non-WDB control group.
- **2022 Aspergillus mycotoxin study** (n=236 ME/CFS): 92.4% had Ochratoxin A — but no control, no general-pop prevalence
- **Tim Henrich UCSF**: mold detected in ~⅓ of [[Long COVID]] patients
- **[[Dr Davis]]**: supports need for more research

## Testing

- Urine mycotoxin panel (not FDA-approved)
- ERMI Swiffer dust test — for the living environment
- HLA-DR typing — for genetic susceptibility (Shoemaker framework)
- C4a, MMP-9, VEGF — CIRS biomarkers

## Treatment angle

[[CIRS]] note for treatment specifics. Generally: remediation, binders (cholestyramine), nasal antifungals, intranasal VIP — out of scope for source.
