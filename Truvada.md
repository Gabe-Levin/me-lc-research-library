# Truvada

HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis (tenofovir disoproxil fumarate + emtricitabine). Now being repurposed for [[EBV]] and [[Long COVID]] via its potent inhibition of EBV polymerase.

## Mechanism for [[EBV]]

**Drosu 2020**: TDF and TAF (tenofovir prodrugs) directly inhibit EBV DNA polymerase. **TAF 35× / TDF 10× more potent than acyclovir** in cell-based EBV assays. TAF also 2× more potent than ganciclovir.

## Studies and trials in progress

- Being studied for [[EBV]] in MS (TAF/TDF block EBV polymerase)
- **Putrino Mt. Sinai LC trial in progress**: Truvada ± [[Maraviroc]] — completion early 2026
- Also has lab-scale activity vs SARS-CoV-2

## Practical

- Strong safety record from 20 years of HIV use
- Renal monitoring (tenofovir nephrotoxicity small risk)
- Off-label use requires open-minded prescriber
