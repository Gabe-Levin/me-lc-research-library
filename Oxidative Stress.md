# Oxidative Stress

Excess reactive oxygen species (ROS) damaging cellular components — particularly mitochondrial membranes. Documented in both [[ME-CFS]] and [[Long COVID]]; correlates with fatigue severity (Paul 2021, Redox Biology).

## Sources in IACCs

- Mitochondrial leakage during inefficient ATP production
- Lactic acid buildup during reverse-running Krebs cycle ([[Post-Exertional Malaise]])
- Chronic immune activation
- Viral persistence (Neubrand 2023)

## Countermeasures (per source)

- Glutathione (liposomal, IV)
- NAC (precursor)
- CoQ10 / Ubiquinol
- MitoQ (mitochondrial-targeted)
- ALA (alpha-lipoic acid)
- Resveratrol
- Vitamin C (high-dose IV — see [[EBV]])
- [[Red Light Therapy]] (mitochondrial-level antioxidant effect)
- [[Methylene Blue]] (reduces electron leakage at Complex I)

Note: [[HBOT]] *may* be contraindicated for latent herpesviruses because increased oxidative stress promotes latent cell expansion — see [[Viral Persistence and Reactivation]].
