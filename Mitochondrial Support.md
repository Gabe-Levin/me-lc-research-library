# Mitochondrial Support

Category note for the supplement/treatment cluster aimed at [[Mitochondrial Dysfunction]].

## Layers

### Antioxidants (reduce mitochondrial ROS)
CoQ10 · MitoQ (targeted) · PQQ · NAC · resveratrol · ALA · liposomal glutathione

### Membrane repair
[[Phosphatidylcholine]] · ATP360 · NT Factor lipids · BioPC Pro · BHC liposomes

### Substrates and cofactors
L-carnitine (ALCAR) · D-ribose · [[NAD Plus]] (NR/NMN/NADH/patches/IV) · B vitamins · magnesium

### Krebs cycle modulation
[[Oxaloacetate]] (SDH inhibition during reverse-running Krebs) · Chrysin (also SDH)

### Electron transport "patch"
[[Methylene Blue]]

### External
[[Red Light Therapy]] / [[Photobiomodulation]] · [[HBOT]] · exercise (PEM-aware only)

Pre-exertion: OAA + Chrysin (block SDH reverse). Post-exertion: PQQ, hydrogen water, red light. Daily: nattokinase, vinpocetine, ginkgo, aspirin, pycnogenol ([[Microclots]]).
